how it works

i.

the manifest is public

Every card in the box is a real TCG single, written to the chain when the box is loaded. You know exactly what can be pulled and what each card trades for on TCGplayer. The only secret is the draw order, decided by commit-reveal at opening time.

ii.

the pool is the vending machine

Packs are a token you buy by swapping native ETH through a Uniswap v4 pool. The hook replaces the AMM curve entirely: no liquidity providers, just a quote derived from the TCGplayer value of the unopened cards and the live ETH price.

iii.

sealed packs stay liquid

No card shop buys back sealed packs at fair value. The hook does, at EV minus a spread. Pulled a bad read on the box? Sell your sealed packs back and walk away.
